Crane is a web platform engine written in Zig: the browser, minus the pixels. This is its record of its own results on the web-platform-tests (WPT), measured by its own runner and regenerated with every run. Scope: the 12,824 testharness files of the 0.1 worklist, in 53 suites. Reftests and tests that need rendering or layout (html/rendering/, canvas, scrolling, focus traversal) are out of scope by design, and every total describes this scope and nothing wider.

About the totals. Crane passes 1,810,840 of the 1,881,944 subtests its runs reported. A file blocks the 0.1 gate when it times out, errors, crashes or finishes with no subtest passing (TIMEOUT, ERROR, CRASH, NONE-PASSED): 1,622 files block today.
