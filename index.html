Status of this document

Crane is a web platform engine written in Zig: the browser, minus the pixels. This is its record of its own results on the web-platform-tests (WPT), measured by its own runner and regenerated with every run. Scope: the 9,953 testharness files of the 0.1 worklist, in 46 suites. Reftests and tests that need rendering or layout (html/rendering/, canvas, scrolling, focus traversal) are out of scope by design, and every total describes this scope and nothing wider.

About the totals. Crane passes 1,669,398 of the 1,675,588 subtests its runs reported. This is not a browser comparison, and the subtest total mostly measures encoding/, which holds 69% of all reported subtests (1,152,027 of 1,675,588) in 152 files. A file blocks the 0.1 gate when it times out, errors, crashes or finishes with no subtest passing (TIMEOUT, ERROR, CRASH, NONE-PASSED): 485 files block today. Per-subtest detail comes from the runner’s result streams: 9,947 of 9,953 files have it in this generation.
