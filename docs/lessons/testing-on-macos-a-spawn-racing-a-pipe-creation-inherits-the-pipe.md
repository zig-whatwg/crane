# Testing: On macOS a spawn racing another thread's pipe creation inherits the pipe

**Date**: 2026-11-21
**Lesson**: Zig 0.17's `std.process.spawn` on macOS makes each stdio pipe with pipe() and only then marks it close-on-exec (Darwin has no pipe2), and calls posix_spawn without POSIX_SPAWN_CLOEXEC_DEFAULT, so a process spawned on another thread in between holds both ends - and the pipe's reader sees end of stream only when that stray holder exits too; a multi-threaded spawner on macOS serializes its spawns.

**Why**: "End of stream" on a pipe means every write end is closed, in every process. Without atomic close-on-exec (Linux's pipe2(O_CLOEXEC)) or CLOEXEC_DEFAULT, a spawn on another thread inside the pipe()-to-fcntl() window copies the descriptor - std/Io/Threaded.zig `spawnDarwin` (Zig master 2ff397f75cf, 2026-11-20, unchanged). Go closes the same window with syscall.ForkLock (src/syscall/exec_unix.go).

**What Happened**: Lane flk3 chased "test runner failed to respond for 1m" with every test passed (wpt_runner-repeat under -Dsanitize-thread, 2 of 5 runs on chat.local; tools-lint_tombstones, 2026-11-03), and suspected this race in zig build's runner. A probe (spawn /usr/bin/true with three pipes from 4 threads beside threads spawning longer-lived processes; after reaping each child, poll a close-on-exec duplicate of its read ends) found it real: on chat2, 13 to 51 of every 2,000 exited children still had a pipe another process held; with every spawn under one lock, 0. The launcher's own worker threads spawn this way: its spawn race test failed 9 of 50 runs on chat2 without a lock, 0 of 50 with one. But in real builds the build runner's spawns were never seen leaking: 1,330 launcher starts that count inherited pipes closed none (chat.local), an lsof sampler over two TSAN runs saw none (chat2), and Zig's own runner spawning 60 short and 8 long test steps in one burst, idle and starved, failed none of 12 builds. So the race is real inside a process that spawns from several threads at full rate; nothing showed it causing that silence.

**Fix**: tools/test_launcher/spawn.zig - the launcher's threads spawn through one `Spawner` lock (the window closed inside the process); and first in main the launcher closes every pipe above stderr that is not close-on-exec, except ZIG_PROGRESS's (what a racing spawn could have handed it; OpenSSH's ssh does the same at start, closefrom(STDERR_FILENO + 1)). Its server-mode summary says when it closed any.

**Takeaway**: **On macOS, serialize every spawn of a multi-threaded process (or use POSIX_SPAWN_CLOEXEC_DEFAULT): std's pipe-then-fcntl leaves a window a racing spawn copies. And prove a race in situ before blaming it: a mechanism a probe shows at full rate can be too rare at a real program's rate to explain the symptom.**
