# Testing: A test of a collector's ceiling reads the space at rest, with values the engine cannot pretenure

**Date**: 2026-11-12
**Lesson**: A test that pins the size of a JavaScript engine's young generation has to make the generation GROW first, and read it when no full collection is marking - both are the collector's policy, not the test's, and each one made a first version of the test wrong in a different direction.

**Why**: The young generation only grows while what it holds survives (V8 15.7.112.43 Heap::ShouldResizeNewSpace: survivors since the last expansion exceed its capacity), so a test of its CEILING must first drive it there. Two policies sit in the way. (1) Allocation-site pretenuring: array and object literals that keep surviving are allocated in the old generation from then on, so the young generation stops growing long before its ceiling. (2) A full collection that is marking lets the young generation take pages ABOVE its ceiling so the marking is not cut short, and returns them afterwards (heap.cc:5552-5580, Heap::ShouldExpandYoungGenerationOnSlowAllocation, IsNewSpaceAllowedToGrowAboveTargetCapacity) - and the very survivors the test made keep full collections marking.

**What Happened**: The tests for `engine.AgentOptions.young_generation_limit` (tests/v8/young_generation_limit_test.zig) went through three shapes. With array literals as the survivors, the UNLIMITED agent stopped at 4 MB semispaces, not V8's 32 MB: the control could not tell a limit from no limit. With strings and closures it reached 32 MB, but a reading taken at the end was 2 MB once where 4 MB was expected (a shrink had just run). Taking the PEAK over the pieces failed the other way: a 24 MB limit read 24.5 MB committed, a 6 MB limit 18.9 MB - a transient above the ceiling while marking, which is V8 working as designed. The version that holds reads twice: the MOST committed at any time (the generation reached its ceiling), and the LEAST committed after pieces of script that make garbage alone (the old generation stops growing, marking finishes, the fast allocation keeps the young generation at its size) - the generation at rest. 10 of 10 and 5 of 5 in verify mode; RED: the three limit tests fail without the adapter's constraint.

**Fix**: (1) Survivors are values no allocation site pretenures: strings built at run time and closures, not literals. (2) A control with NO limit proves the workload reaches the engine's own ceiling - without it a "limit respected" result may be a generation that never grew. (3) Read at rest: after garbage-only work, the least of several readings; never one reading, and never the peak. (4) State the transient in the option's documentation ("at rest"), because a host will see it too.

**Takeaway**: **To pin a collector's limit: drive the space to its ceiling with values the engine cannot pretenure, prove that with an unlimited control, and compare the limit against the space AT REST (the least committed after garbage-only work) - a single reading catches a shrink, and a peak catches the pages a marking collection is allowed above the ceiling.**
